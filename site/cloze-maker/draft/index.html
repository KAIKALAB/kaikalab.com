Draft testing

You are reading this because you were invited to try Cloze Maker before it was published.

What a draft version is

Draft testers see an unpublished version of the add-on and its Marketplace listing. It is the same software everyone else will get, shown early so that problems turn up before release rather than after it.

How to stop

Two things are separate, and you can do either or both.

  • Stop using the add-on. Open any Google Docs document, go to Extensions, find Cloze Maker, and uninstall it. You can also remove its access to your account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Stop being a draft tester. Write to support@kaikalab.com and ask to be removed. We will take your address off the list and confirm by reply. You do not have to give a reason.

Your data

Nothing changes for draft testers: the passages you work on are never stored, and the usage we record is counts against an anonymous identifier. The privacy policy covers it in full, and leaving the draft does not leave anything of yours behind.
