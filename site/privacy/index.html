Privacy Policy — KaikaLab Content Publisher

Last updated: November 24, 2026

KaikaLab Content Publisher ("the App") is a content publishing tool operated by Kaika Lab (Osaka, Japan) for Kaika Lab's language-learning services, including TOPIKPILOT, JLPT-GO, and HSK-GO. This policy explains what information the App handles when authorized creators use it to publish content to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Information we collect

When a creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit), we receive and store only:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information (open ID, username, display name, and avatar) obtained through the user.info.basic scope;
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok with the creator's consent, used to call the Content Posting API;
  • Records of content the creator chose to publish through the App (video file reference, title, caption, privacy level, and publication status).

We do not collect TikTok passwords. Authentication is handled entirely by TikTok.

How we use the information

  • To verify the creator's identity and display their TikTok username and avatar on the posting screen before publishing;
  • To upload and publish, at the creator's direction, educational videos to the creator's TikTok account (scopes video.upload and video.publish);
  • To maintain publication records for the operation of Kaika Lab's official accounts.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use TikTok user data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on Kaika Lab's secured server, are never embedded in client-side code, and are accessible only to authorized Kaika Lab operators.

Revoking access and deletion

Creators may revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Manage permissions). To request deletion of stored tokens and publication records, contact us at support@kaikalab.com. Upon revocation or a deletion request, stored tokens are deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features change. The current version will always be available at this URL.
