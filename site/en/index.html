Our Projects
01

TOPIK PILOT

Korean study site and app

A free study site for Korean, from first steps up to TOPIK II, with news reading and listening practice every morning and word lists for levels 1 to 6. Written for Japanese speakers, with English pages for the word lists and daily practice. There is also an Android app for TOPIK II.

02

JLPT-GO

Practice tests for the JLPT

Practice tests for JLPT N1, N2 and N3. Every question comes with an explanation of why the answer is right. It is available on Google Play.

04

Cloze Maker for Japanese and Korean

A tool for teachers (Google Docs™ add-on)

Paste a passage and get a cloze worksheet and an answer key. Japanese has no spaces between words, so it runs proper morphological analysis instead of splitting on spaces. For Korean it separates particles so you can blank them on their own.
