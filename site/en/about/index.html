About Kaika Lab

About us

Kaika Lab is an independent software studio in Osaka, Japan. Michael Jiang founded it in July 2026. We make exam-prep apps and study materials for people learning Korean and Japanese, and tools for the teachers who teach them.

How the business works

Business model

Our main source of revenue is subscriptions to our exam-prep apps. TOPIK and JLPT scores are used in university admission and hiring, so learners have a clear reason to pay for practice tests and explanations before exam day.

The free study site, word lists and drama episodes are where learners first find us. From there we point them to the apps, where they can practise in exam format.

Cloze Maker is free for now. We are measuring installs and repeat use before deciding on paid features. JLPT-GO also starts with free practice tests, and a paid plan will follow.

Kaika Lab is run by its founder, and our servers run on cloud services that charge by usage. This keeps our fixed costs low.

AI in our products

Claude API

We plan to use the Claude API inside our products: for an AI tutor that explains grammar and answers learners' questions in English, Japanese and Korean, and for a pipeline that drafts and checks practice questions and explanations before they go to human review.
